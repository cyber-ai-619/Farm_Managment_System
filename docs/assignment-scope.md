# Assignment Scope

This repository reflects the Full Farm Management System brief in a structured way so the team can work module by module.

## Broad requirement groups from the brief

- farm and field management
- crop management
- irrigation and water management
- livestock management
- farm inventory and inputs
- farm equipment and machinery
- labour and employee management
- pest and disease management
- weather and environmental monitoring
- harvest management
- market and sales management
- financial management
- supplier and procurement management
- storage and post-harvest management
- dashboard and analytics
- notifications and alerts
- reports and analytics
- security and user management

## Current project stance

- The backend is plain PHP.
- The frontend is a static starter.
- The repo is a scaffold, not a completed product.
- Team members can add implementation detail without reorganizing the repository.
